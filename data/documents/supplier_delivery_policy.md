# Supplier Delivery and Escalation Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how procurement teams should manage supplier delivery delays and
escalate purchase orders that are at risk of affecting business operations.

## Delivery monitoring

Purchase orders must be reviewed when the expected delivery date is missed and the order
has not been delivered. Procurement should verify the current order status and collect
available evidence from supplier communications and service incidents.

A delayed purchase order should not automatically be attributed to a specific root cause.
The investigation must distinguish confirmed evidence from assumptions.

## Supplier escalation

When multiple delayed purchase orders are associated with the same supplier, procurement
should determine whether the delays indicate a broader supplier-level issue.

Supplier escalation is required when a recurring delivery problem affects multiple active
orders or when the delay creates a material operational risk.

The escalation should include the affected purchase orders, known supplier incidents,
supporting evidence, and any unresolved information.

## Investigation outcome

The investigation should clearly identify confirmed facts, possible contributing factors,
and missing evidence. If the available information is insufficient to determine the root
cause, the result must explicitly state that the cause could not be confirmed.
