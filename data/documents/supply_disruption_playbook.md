# Supplier Capacity and Supply Disruption Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook provides guidance for investigating supply disruptions caused by supplier
capacity constraints, component shortages, or other availability problems.

## Capacity constraints

When a supplier reports insufficient production capacity, procurement should identify the
active purchase orders that may be affected and assess whether committed delivery dates
remain achievable.

A supplier-level capacity incident may indicate risk across several orders, but it should
not be treated as proof that every delayed order has the same root cause. Order-specific
evidence should be preferred when available.

## Component shortages

When a supplier reports a component shortage, the investigation should identify the
affected purchase orders, the unavailable component when known, and any revised delivery
commitment provided by the supplier.

## Recommended actions

For unresolved supply disruptions, procurement should request an updated delivery plan
from the supplier and prioritize orders according to operational impact.

The investigation should distinguish confirmed supply constraints from potential risks
that have not yet been verified.
