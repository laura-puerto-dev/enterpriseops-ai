# Incoming Quality Inspection Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure defines the actions required when incoming supplier material is subject to
quality inspection or a quality-related hold.

## Inspection hold

Material affected by a quality concern must remain on hold until the required inspection
has been completed. Procurement and operations should not treat the material as available
for normal use while the hold remains active.

When a purchase order is affected, the investigation should identify the relevant quality
incident and determine whether the inspection is preventing the order from progressing.

## Resolution

The quality team should record the inspection outcome and any required corrective action.
The purchase order may proceed when the material has been accepted or the applicable
quality restriction has been removed.

If the inspection outcome is still pending, the investigation should report the quality
hold as unresolved rather than assuming that the material will be accepted.
